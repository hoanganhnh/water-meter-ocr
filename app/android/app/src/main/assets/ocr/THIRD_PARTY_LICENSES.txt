Water Meter OCR — Bundled OCR Assets & Runtime Notices (supplied-yolo-3stage-v1)
================================================================================

1. Bundled ONNX Models (`assets/ocr/`)
--------------------------------------
- `frame.onnx` (exported from supplied `models/best_latest.pt`, YOLOv8n-OBB)
- `reverse.onnx` (exported from supplied `models/best_1608.pt`, YOLOv8n detect)
- `digits.onnx` (exported from supplied `models/best_0608.pt`, YOLOv8n detect)
- `supplied-yolo-manifest.json`

These ONNX model files are deterministic FP32 opset-17 exports of the project-supplied
water-meter checkpoints (`best_latest.pt`, `best_1608.pt`, `best_0608.pt`) trained with
Ultralytics YOLOv8. Model architectures derive from Ultralytics YOLOv8 (AGPL-3.0 / Enterprise
license terms apply per upstream Ultralytics licensing; weights are proprietary project
deliverables supplied for this repository).
